Reactivating a canceled subscription charges every missed billing cycle

Steps
  1. Create a monthly subscription with a July billing date and cancel it.
  2. Freeze the clock in late September.
  3. Reactivate the subscription and run the renewal sweep.

On reactivation, move the billing date to the latest cycle already due, using a small shared date helper.
